# EdgeFlow Beta Test Plan
**Duration:** 2 weeks  
**Goal:** Validate tier design, find friction points, and gather enough signal to finalize pricing before payment integration.

---

## Recruitment

- Target: Discord trading communities (prop firm, funded account, ICT/SMC traders)
- Profile: traders who already journal or want to — not casual traders
- Ask: "Use it for 2 weeks, log real trades, give me 5 minutes of feedback at the end"
- Free access: they already have it (free tier is open)

---

## What to Watch

- Do users hit the 50-trade cap?
- Do users use all 3 AI Advisor messages?
- Which pages do they actually visit? (Dashboard, AI, Analyst, Leak Detection)
- Which features do they ignore entirely?
- Are any Kenyan traders signing up? (determines if Intasend is worth building in v1)

---

## Feedback Form Questions

Send this at the end of the 2 weeks (Google Form / Tally):

1. How many trades did you log during the beta?
2. Which features did you actually use? (checkboxes: Dashboard, AI Advisor, Leak Detection, Optimizer, CSV Import, PDF Export, Trading Plan)
3. Which features did you never open?
4. What would make you stop using this and go back to what you used before?
5. What's the one thing that would make you pay for this?
6. What's broken or confusing that you worked around?
7. How would you rate the AI Advisor? (1-5) What did it get right? What did it miss?
8. Would you recommend this to another trader? Why / why not?
9. What's your trading setup? (prop firm / funded / personal account)
10. Are you based in Kenya? (helps with payment method decision)

---

## After Beta

Based on feedback:
- Adjust free tier limits if cap is too aggressive or too generous
- Cut features nobody used
- Double down on features they loved
- Decide if Intasend (Kenya M-Pesa) is worth v1 or can wait
- Then build payment integration (Lemon Squeezy + optionally Intasend)
- Then pricing page
